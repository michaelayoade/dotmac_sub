# Customer chargeability review

Use **Admin → Customers → Billing → Non-billable / review** as the complete
operational queue.

1. Read the row badge. **Confirmed non-billable** needs no pricing repair.
   **Review required** is not a free-service decision.
2. Open Customer 360 and inspect every current subscription, its offer/version,
   active recurring prices, contracted unit price, and any effective billing
   treatment.
3. For a missing price, add or activate exactly one authoritative recurring
   catalog price. For duplicates, retire the incorrect active price. For a
   catalog/subscription mismatch, repair the owning contract or catalog record;
   do not set a zero merely to remove the review badge.
4. Refresh the customer list and confirm the account moves to Confirmed
   non-billable, Prepaid, or Postpaid as the repaired evidence requires.
5. If a genuinely free account is disabled, run or wait for the permanent
   billing-approval reconciliation. It restores only billing-owned disables.
   Escalate any security/admin disable or unrelated enforcement lock to its
   owner.
6. Before a billing-mode conversion, resolve every blocker shown in the staff
   preview. Confirm only after reviewing the target mode, affected services,
   billing boundary, currency, funding, and preserved receivables.

Never remove invoices, credit, paid coverage, billing anchors, or access locks
to make a filter or conversion appear successful.
